Cantonese is an integral part of Chinese culture and my mother tongue. Cantonese-speaking communities have played an important role in China’s modern history.

I am committed to promoting Cantonese through community engagement. In 2025, I founded YueVoice, a Cantonese club at my high school.

Technology can serve both as a tool for promoting Cantonese and as a means of deepening our understanding of linguistics.

Inspired by an AI demonstration of Cantonese pronunciation assessment, I led the design of an interactive website that combines classic Cantonese storytelling with Cantonese read-aloud exercises using Jyutping romanization system. This project turns phonetic theory into a practical tool for language learning and cultural engagement.
