I was selected for the Young Talent (Yingcai) Program, a nationwide program sponsored by the China Association for Science and Technology (CAST) that provides high school students with research training at leading universities across China. 

As a member of the Computer Science Group, I gained an overview of modern and emerging computing technologies, completed a literature review, and laid the groundwork for a team project to develop a Cantonese learning website with audio features.
